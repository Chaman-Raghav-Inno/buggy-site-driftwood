How pricing works

What you pay

  • Nights. Each listing has a weekday rate (Sunday to Thursday nights) and a weekend rate (Friday and Saturday nights). A night belongs to the date you check in: checking in on Friday means a Friday night.
  • Stay discounts. 10% off the nights for stays of 7 nights or more; 20% off for 28 nights or more.
  • Extra guests. Every listing includes 2 guests. Each additional guest aged 13 or over costs $25 a night. Children aged 2 to 12 stay free but count towards the listing's maximum; infants under 2 are free and don't count.
  • Pets. $40 per stay, at listings that allow pets, up to the listing's pet limit.
  • Cleaning fee. Set by the host, once per stay.
  • Service fee. 12% of the nights (after any stay discount) plus extra-guest fees. It is never refunded.
  • Taxes. The local occupancy tax on the nights (after discounts), extra-guest fees and the cleaning fee: Honolulu 13.96%, Tokyo 10.35%, Reykjavík 11.5%.
  • Promo codes. STAY15 takes 15% off the nights (after any stay discount) for stays of 3 nights or more. Expired codes can't be used.

Dates and cancellations

  • Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out by 11:00 AM, local time at the listing. You can check in on the day another guest checks out.
  • Minimum stay. Some listings need a minimum number of nights. Every stay is at least 1 night.
  • Flexible: full refund until 24 hours before check-in (3:00 PM local time). No refund after that.
  • Moderate: full refund until 5 days before check-in. After that, 50% of the nightly amount.
  • Strict: 50% of the nightly amount until 7 days before check-in. No refund after that.
  • Refunds never include the service fee. Changing your dates re-prices your stay.
  • Currencies. Prices are set in US dollars. Choosing euros or yen shows converted amounts (1 USD = 0.9187 EUR = 151.37 JPY).
